    Settings & backup

    Your answers are saved on this phone, inside this app only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it works without internet. Deleting the app from your home screen erases them, so export a backup now and then.

    Moving your data? The home screen app, Safari, and your computer each keep their own copy and start empty. Export a backup where your data is now, then open the app and use Import backup. On iPhone, Export opens the share sheet, so pick "Save to Files".
